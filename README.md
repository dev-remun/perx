
## perx

`perx` is my work for the **Build your own shell** in the [build-your-own-x](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) challenges by the **Codecrafters**.

The term 'perx' comes from the name of Perry the Platypus from the show Phineas and Ferb. I shortened and lowercased the word 'Perry' and added 'x' to mean execution, coming up with the term 'perx'.

This work is purely experimental and an exploration to revive the programming knowledge in C that was taught to us during our 1st year of college. So, this project will contain some `.c` and executable files that are intended to help me recall some C syntax and concepts.

Also, I cloned a different GitHub repo given by Codecrafters, where I submit my solutions for their challenges and they test every submission which reflects on my profile.
